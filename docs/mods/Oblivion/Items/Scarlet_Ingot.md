## Description
The scarlet ingot is another somewhat rare metal ingot. It can be crafted by putting 6 [raw scarlet](/wiki/mods/Oblivion/Items/Raw_Scarlet) together and is used to craft [items](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Item) and [tools](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Tool).

## Obtaining
<div id="crafting-table">
<div class="crafting-element" crafting-type="vanilla_crafting">
input[
    2=oblivion:raw_scarlet, Raw Scarlet, Oblivion/Items/Raw_Scarlet, true;
    4=oblivion:raw_scarlet, Raw Scarlet, Oblivion/Items/Raw_Scarlet, true;
    5=oblivion:raw_scarlet, Raw Scarlet, Oblivion/Items/Raw_Scarlet, true;
    6=oblivion:raw_scarlet, Raw Scarlet, Oblivion/Items/Raw_Scarlet, true
]
output[oblivion:scarlet_ingot, Scarlet Ingot]
</div>
<div class="crafting-element" crafting-type="vanilla_crafting">
input[
    5=oblivion:scarlet_block, Block of Scarlet, Oblivion/Blocks/Scarlet_Block, true
]
output[oblivion:raw_scarlet, Raw Scarlet ]
</div>
</div>
