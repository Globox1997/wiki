## Description
Raw solarite is a rare material. It can be gathered by drilling a [block of raw solarite](/wiki/mods/Oblivion/Blocks/Raw_Solarite_Block). A [drill](/wiki/mods/Oblivion/Blocks/Drill) is required to not break the raw solarite block.

## Obtaining
A [drill](/wiki/mods/Oblivion/Blocks/Drill) is required to not break the [block of raw solarite](/wiki/mods/Oblivion/Blocks/Raw_Solarite_Block).
