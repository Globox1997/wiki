## Description
The fiery netherite ingot is a somewhat rare metal ingot. It can be crafted by putting 4 pieces of [fiery netherite scrap](/wiki/mods/Oblivion/Items/Fiery_Netherite_Scrap) together and is used to craft [items](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Item) and [tools](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Tool).

## Obtaining
<div id="crafting-table">
<div class="crafting-element" crafting-type="vanilla_crafting">
input[
    2=oblivion:fiery_netherite_scrap, Fiery Netherite Scrap, Oblivion/Items/Fiery_Netherite_Scrap, true;
    4=oblivion:fiery_netherite_scrap, Fiery Netherite Scrap, Oblivion/Items/Fiery_Netherite_Scrap, true;
    5=oblivion:fiery_netherite_scrap, Fiery Netherite Scrap, Oblivion/Items/Fiery_Netherite_Scrap, true;
    6=oblivion:fiery_netherite_scrap, Fiery Netherite Scrap, Oblivion/Items/Fiery_Netherite_Scrap, true
]
output[oblivion:fiery_netherite_ingot, Fiery Netherite Ingot]
</div>
</div>
