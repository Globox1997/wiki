## Description
Elysian bone meal is an [item](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Item) that can cause many [plants](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Plant) and plantlike blocks to instantly grow when used on them by hand or via a [dispenser](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Dispenser). If used on [grass blocks](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Grass_Block) [shimmering grass](/wiki/mods/Oblivion/Blocks/Shimmering_Grass) will appear.

## Usage
Elysian bone meal can be used on the plants in this [list](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Bone_Meal#Fertilizer) to have a chance to increase their growth stage or spread them.
