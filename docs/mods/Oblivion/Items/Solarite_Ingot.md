## Description
The solarite ingot is another somewhat rare metal ingot. It can be crafted by putting 9 [raw solarite](/wiki/mods/Oblivion/Items/Raw_Solarite) together and is used to craft [items](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Item) and [tools](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Tool).
